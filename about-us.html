Our Story

Where Inspiration Meets Creation

BIKO Industries was founded on a simple idea: every business deserves access to premium packaging without the complexity or cost that typically comes with it. For over a decade, we've been delivering factory-direct packaging solutions that help companies of all sizes stand out, protect their products, and operate more efficiently.

Headquartered in Cape Coral, Florida, we maintain a fully stocked warehouse in Chicago, Illinois and leverage long-standing overseas relationships to source packaging directly from manufacturers in China. This global reach allows us to offer highly competitive pricing without sacrificing the quality and reliability our clients expect.

What sets BIKO apart isn't just the products we offer — it's the way we work with our clients. We believe that packaging is more than a container; it's the first impression your product makes. That's why we invest in understanding your business, your brand, and your goals before recommending any solution.

Whether you're a startup sourcing your first custom packaging run or an established enterprise looking to streamline warehousing, our team delivers the expertise, responsiveness, and care that larger suppliers simply can't match.
