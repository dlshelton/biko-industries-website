Warehousing Services

At BIKO, we recognize that every business has unique warehousing and packaging needs. That's why we offer custom warehousing plans tailored to your specific requirements. Whether you need specialized packaging storage or a more general solution, we provide the flexibility and support to help streamline your operations.

We proudly operate a warehouse in Chicago, offering fast, reliable deliveries. Additionally, we can provide storage for your packaging products in our China warehouse, helping you save on warehousing costs while ensuring your products are ready for delivery when you need them.

Our team works closely with you to create a personalized warehousing plan that aligns with your business needs. Whether you require storage in our Chicago or China warehouse, inventory management, or fast shipping, we ensure a smooth and efficient process.
